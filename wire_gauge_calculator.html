Why Doesn't Downloaded HTML Run on iPhone & How to Fix

⚠️ The Apple iOS "Quick Look" Restriction

When you download an HTML file on an iPhone and tap it in WhatsApp, Email, or the Files app, iOS opens it in Apple's "Quick Look" previewer. Apple deliberately disables all JavaScript execution inside Quick Look for security. Because this calculator uses JavaScript to calculate wire areas and parallel combinations, it cannot respond inside Quick Look.

✅ How to Run It Smoothly on iPhone (2 Easy Ways):

1 Recommended: Add to iPhone Home Screen (1-Tap Offline App)
  1. Open the web link in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (box with an arrow pointing up 📤).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right.

✨ A "Wire Calc" app icon will be created on your iPhone screen that opens full screen and works offline without internet!

2 If Opening Downloaded .html File: Use "Documents by Readdle"

Download the free "Documents by Readdle" or "HTML Viewer" app from the iOS App Store. Share the downloaded wire_gauge_calculator.html to Documents. Tapping it opens it in a full WebKit browser with complete JavaScript support!
